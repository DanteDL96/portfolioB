Front-End Development


Building responsive, accessible, and well-structured digital content for web and learning platforms.


Front-End Experience

I use front-end development to create digital content that is structured, responsive, and easy to navigate. Through HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, I develop and improve layouts that support clear content organization and consistent experiences across different screen sizes.


Much of my work has involved adapting front-end solutions for web-based learning platforms and online documentation. I focus on selecting practical approaches that work within the requirements and limitations of each platform while keeping the content visually consistent and user-friendly.


Accessibility and usability are central to this work. I apply semantic structure, logical heading hierarchies, responsive layouts, and WCAG principles to create content that is easier to understand, navigate, and use.

Multimedia
Design


Creating purposeful visuals and media that strengthen communication, accessibility, and learner engagement.


Multimedia Design Experience

I create multimedia that supports the purpose of the content rather than serving only as decoration. Using tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, PowerPoint, and Word, I transform ideas into polished visuals, layouts, and media assets for digital learning and communication materials.


I adapt my design approach to the platform, audience, and project requirements. This includes maintaining visual consistency, improving information hierarchy, and selecting media that helps users understand and engage with the content.


I also use modern and AI-assisted tools when appropriate to explore ideas, refine visual assets, and make the creative process more efficient. Throughout the process, I consider accessibility, readability, and how each visual contributes to the overall user experience.


eLearning Development


Developing accessible and engaging learning experiences for LMS and SCORM-based environments.


eLearning Development Experience

I develop and improve digital learning experiences by combining clear content structure, accessible design, multimedia, and front-end development. My goal is to make each course intuitive to navigate, visually consistent, and effective for the learner.


My experience includes creating and adapting content for different eLearning platforms, LMS environments, and SCORM-based formats. I consider the technical requirements of each platform while organizing the learning experience around the needs of the audience and the objectives of the project.


I focus on accessible media, clear layouts, logical progression, and simple interactions that support learning without creating unnecessary complexity. This approach allows me to deliver reliable, user-focused content that meets client requirements and provides learners with a consistent experience.
